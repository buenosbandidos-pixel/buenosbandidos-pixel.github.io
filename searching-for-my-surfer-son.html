Searching for My Surfer Son

Emma turns 44 in San Fernando, near Cádiz. She runs a small craft shop, has never been to Madrid, and is afraid of planes, boats, cars and motorbikes. Then a young policeman who grew up with her son brings a message from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Gastón, a diving instructor and surfer in Thailand, has been sentenced in Chiang Rai to 28 years for drug possession. He refuses all help. Nobody understands why.

Emma flies to the other side of the world with her sister Glòria, a hairdresser who likes karaoke more than travelling. A Finnish fixer who is more trouble than help, the wrong island, money lost at sea and a kind widower from Phuket lead her at last to one hour alone with her son, and to a question she did not expect.

Searching for My Surfer Son is a warm and funny film about a hard subject: two sisters on the road, a love story in middle age, and a mother who has never been anywhere going as far as she can.
